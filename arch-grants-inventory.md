professionals who need structured, reliable information to do their jobs ethically and efficiently.

Communicate more effectively with guided drafting and clarity-focused message support
Organize and document events using structured logs and repeatable templates

screenshots, scattered notes, and ad-hoc templates that don’t scale or hold up under pressure.

Briefly describe the primary market your business serves and your target customer?

tools; (2) attorneys, mediators, therapists, and other providers who want standardized, review-ready records for
faster, safer case work. Initial focus is Missouri with a national expansion path.
Who are your direct and indirect competitors? What is your unique value proposition?
Direct competitors include co-parenting communication platforms like OurFamilyWizard, TalkingParents, AppClose,
2houses, and Custody X Change. Indirect competitors include attorneys’ ad-hoc templates, spreadsheets,
email/text threads, and generic note/CRM tools. Unique value: CoTrackPro combines child-centered, trauma-
informed workflows with AI-assisted drafting, structured logging, and report/PDF outputs across legal, mental-
health, and safety needs—reducing conflict and saving professional review time.
