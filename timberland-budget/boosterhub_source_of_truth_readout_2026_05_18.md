## Dashboard redesign implication

Rebuild the dashboard around a live workshop flow:
- Source-of-truth import status and period selector.
- Operating view excluding trip/pass-through toggles.
- Budget-builder table by major category with columns: 24-25 actual, 25-26 budget, 25-26 actual/YTD, proposed 26-27, owner, confidence, decision needed.
- Decision levers: student fee assumption, fundraising target, known commitments, director requests, reserve contribution/use.
- Variance triage: only material lines, grouped into revenue misses, expense overruns, underspend/carryforward, and coding questions.
- Activity bucket tabs: Marching Band, Fall Color Guard, Winter Guard, Indoor Percussion, Trip, Fundraising, Uncategorized.
