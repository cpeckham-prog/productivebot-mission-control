## What changed

The prior dashboard over-weighted 2024-25 actuals and the 2025-26 budget structure, but missed the live 2025-26 YTD actuals. That omission materially changes the story. The 2025-26 year-to-date report shows:

- **Total income:** budget $144,270.63 / actual $232,891.50 / variance +$88,620.87.
- **Total expense:** budget $144,270.63 / actual $242,501.45 / variance +$98,230.82.
- **Net income:** actual **-$9,609.95** through 4/29/26.
- **Trip income/expense dominate the headline totals:** Trip Income $113,464.28 and Trip Expense $112,851.33. These should be treated as pass-through/activity-specific, not normal operating signal.
- **Membership dues are far below the budget structure:** $38,815.25 actual vs $78,075 budget, driven by Membership Fees $27,367 vs $62,775. This either reflects timing, incomplete collection/classification, or a fee-model assumption that is not translating cleanly into actuals.
- **Fundraising is less dire than 2024-25 but still below plan:** $34,950.17 actual vs $52,838.91 budget, with activity-specific lines now visible: Midwest Marching Percussion Classic $12,169 and Trivia Night $6,113.
- **Donations/grants are materially above budget:** Contributions/Gifts/Grants $25,841.90 actual vs $1,956.60 budget. That is helpful but should be tagged by restriction/confidence before using as operating baseline.
- **Uniform/competition top spend is real and above budget:** Marching Competition Uniforms $14,089.50 vs $12,640 and Fall Color Guard Uniforms $5,905.46 vs $5,500.
- **Credit-card/payment friction is material:** Credit Card Fees income $2,456.10 and expense $2,532.60 appear as unbudgeted/underbudgeted mechanics.

## Revised interpretation

The story is not simply “fundraising was bad in 2024-25, so be conservative.” The fuller story is:

1. The budget is mixing operating activity with very large pass-through/trip cash flows.
2. The new 2025-26 structure still does not reconcile cleanly to actuals.
3. Membership fee assumptions need immediate validation because actuals are far below the model.
4. Fundraising has activity-specific success, but it needs owner/date/net-profit tagging.
5. The board view must separate unrestricted operating economics from trip/pass-through/restricted schedules.
