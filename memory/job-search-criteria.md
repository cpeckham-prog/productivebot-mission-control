# Job Search Criteria — Ladders + LinkedIn Monitoring

## Target Roles
- COO, Head of Operations, VP/SVP Operations
- VP Revenue, CRO, Head of Revenue
- VP/SVP Sales (if leadership-heavy, not IC)
- Other executive leadership in ops/sales/revenue/GTM
- **No individual contributor roles**

## Compensation
- Base: $250k+ minimum
- Must include variable comp (bonus/commission) and equity
- Total comp matters — strong equity can offset slightly lower base

## Company Preferences
- **Prefer:** Non-public growth-stage companies (Series B-D, pre-IPO)
- **Open to:** Various sizes, but sweet spot is scaling companies
- **Industries:** Tech, SaaS, software+hardware, fintech, AI-enabled business
- **Avoid:** Healthcare, insurance

## Location
- Remote: ✅ always fine
- Hybrid: ✅ open to it
- Relocation: ✅ for the right role
- Current: St. Louis (family reasons for staying, but flexible)

## Apply Strategy
- **Auto-apply / strong pursue:** Clear fits — right level, right industry, right comp, company looks solid
- **Flag for review:** Interesting but borderline — unfamiliar company, slightly off-title, might want custom approach
- **Skip:** IC roles, healthcare/insurance, clearly below comp threshold, early-stage (<Series A) unless exceptional

## LinkedIn Triage Standard
Use the same three-way screen on LinkedIn results before surfacing anything.

### Clear fit
Surface as a strong candidate when most of the following are true:
- Executive or near-executive level: COO, CRO, CBO, Head of Operations, Head of Revenue, VP/SVP Ops, VP/SVP Revenue, Head of Business Operations, Head of Strategic Operations, VP Revenue Operations
- Scope matches Chris's background: company-building, cross-functional leadership, GTM + operations, deployment/delivery, customer operations, field/service operations, business operations, revenue operations, post-M&A integration, or broad commercial leadership
- Company profile looks directionally right: tech, SaaS, software+hardware, fintech, AI-enabled business, or adjacent modern operating environment
- Compensation appears likely to be in range (prefer $250K+ base, or strong total comp with bonus/equity)
- Seniority is real: reports into CEO/President/CRO or owns a meaningful company-wide function
- Not obviously a narrow specialist role disguised as leadership

### Borderline / review
Flag for review instead of treating as a top hit when any of these are true:
- Title is adjacent but not perfect: GM, President, Chief Business Officer, commercial ops, customer ops, deployment/delivery ops, strategic programs, transformation, or chief of staff to CEO with real operating scope
- Compensation is not listed but company/role otherwise looks credible
- Company is solid but industry fit is imperfect
- Role is senior but function is narrower than ideal (e.g. rev ops-heavy, support-heavy, services-heavy, or product-ops-heavy)
- Hybrid/on-site could work but would need judgment on travel or relocation
- PE-backed / turnaround / builder role where the title fits but context needs a closer read

### Skip
Do not surface except in unusual cases:
- Individual contributor roles
- Manager / senior manager / director-level roles unless clearly mis-leveled and actually executive in scope
- Healthcare and insurance by default
- Pure manufacturing / plant / warehouse / store operations roles unless something unusually compelling overrides it
- Pure sales roles without broader leadership scope
- Narrow specialist roles: people ops, marketing ops, sales ops without enterprise leadership scope, finance ops, product ops, IT ops, or procurement-only roles
- Compensation clearly below target
- Tiny early-stage companies (<Series A / very small businesses) unless the role is exceptional and the economics are unusually strong
- Anything that reads as title inflation without meaningful ownership
