## ARTICLE I — FORMATION AND NAME

**1.1 Formation.** The Company was formed as a limited liability company in the State of Missouri by filing Articles of Organization with the Missouri Secretary of State on May 7, 2026, pursuant to the Missouri Revised Statutes, Chapter 347 (the "Act").

**1.2 Name.** The name of the Company is **CPD Operating Group, LLC**. The Company may conduct business under that name or any other name approved by the Member.

**1.3 Duration.** The Company shall have perpetual existence unless dissolved in accordance with this Agreement or the Act.

## ARTICLE V — CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

**5.4 Return of Capital.** The Member has no right to withdraw or be repaid any capital contribution except as provided in this Agreement or required by law.

---

## ARTICLE VI — ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**6.1 Allocations.** All profits, losses, and other tax items of the Company shall be allocated 100% to the Member.

**6.2 Distributions.** The Member may cause the Company to make distributions at any time and in any amount, subject to the restrictions of the Act. All distributions shall be made 100% to the Member.

---

## ARTICLE VII — MANAGEMENT

**7.1 Member-Managed.** The Company shall be member-managed. The Member shall have full and exclusive authority to manage the business and affairs of the Company.

**7.2 Powers of the Member.** The Member shall have the power and authority to take any action on behalf of the Company that is not prohibited by the Act or this Agreement, including but not limited to:

- (a) Entering into, executing, and delivering contracts, agreements, and other instruments;
- (b) Borrowing money and issuing evidences of indebtedness, and securing the same by mortgage, pledge, or other encumbrance of Company assets;
- (c) Acquiring, holding, managing, and disposing of real and personal property;
- (d) Hiring employees, independent contractors, agents, and advisors;
- (e) Opening and maintaining bank and investment accounts;
- (f) Commencing, prosecuting, defending, or settling legal proceedings;
- (g) Making all tax elections and filings on behalf of the Company;
- (h) Doing all other acts necessary, appropriate, or incidental to the Company's business.

**7.3 Delegation.** The Member may delegate authority to officers, employees, agents, or other representatives as the Member deems appropriate.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — BANKING

**8.1 Bank Accounts.** The Company shall maintain one or more accounts at financial institutions as determined by the Member. All Company funds shall be deposited in such accounts and shall not be commingled with the personal funds of the Member.

**8.2 Authorized Signatories.** The Member, and any person designated by the Member, shall be authorized signatories on all Company accounts.

---

## ARTICLE IX — LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Limitation of Liability.** To the fullest extent permitted by the Act and other applicable law, the Member shall not be personally liable for any debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company, whether arising in contract, tort, or otherwise, solely by reason of being a member or manager of the Company.

**9.2 Indemnification.** The Company shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Member, and any officer, employee, or agent of the Company (each, an "Indemnified Person"), to the fullest extent permitted by law, against any losses, claims, damages, liabilities, expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees), judgments, fines, settlements, and other amounts arising from any action, suit, or proceeding related to the Indemnified Person's role with the Company, provided that the Indemnified Person acted in good faith and in a manner reasonably believed to be in or not opposed to the best interests of the Company, and provided further that no indemnification shall be made in respect of any matter as to which the Indemnified Person shall have been adjudicated to have acted with willful misconduct or fraud.

**9.3 Expenses.** The Company may advance expenses incurred by an Indemnified Person in defending any proceeding to the fullest extent permitted by law.

---

## ARTICLE X — BOOKS, RECORDS, AND ACCOUNTING

**10.1 Books and Records.** The Company shall maintain complete and accurate books and records of its business and affairs at its principal office. Such records shall include:

- (a) A current list of the name and address of the Member;
- (b) Copies of the Articles of Organization and all amendments;
- (c) Copies of this Agreement and all amendments;
- (d) Copies of all federal, state, and local tax returns and financial statements for the three most recent fiscal years;
- (e) Minutes of any formal actions or resolutions of the Member.

**10.2 Fiscal Year.** The fiscal year of the Company shall be the calendar year unless the Member elects otherwise.

**10.3 Accounting Method.** The Company shall use the method of accounting determined by the Member for both book and tax purposes.

---

## ARTICLE XI — TAX MATTERS

**11.1 Tax Classification.** The Company, as a single-member LLC, shall be treated as a disregarded entity for federal income tax purposes under Treasury Regulation § 301.7701-3. The Member shall report all items of income, gain, loss, deduction, and credit of the Company on the Member's personal federal income tax return.

**11.2 State Tax Treatment.** The Company shall follow the same tax treatment for Missouri state income tax purposes as applicable under state law.

**11.3 Election to Change Classification.** The Member reserves the right to elect to have the Company treated as a corporation for federal tax purposes and subsequently elect S corporation status under Section 1362 of the Internal Revenue Code, or to make any other tax election, at any time and in the Member's sole discretion.

**11.4 Tax Returns and Filings.** The Member shall be responsible for the preparation and filing of all tax returns and other tax-related documents for the Company.

---

## ARTICLE XII — TRANSFER OF MEMBERSHIP INTEREST

**12.1 Transfers.** The Member may freely transfer all or any portion of the Member's interest in the Company to any person or entity. Any transferee admitted as a substitute member shall be bound by this Agreement.

---

## ARTICLE XIII — DISSOLUTION

**13.1 Events of Dissolution.** The Company shall be dissolved upon the earliest occurrence of any of the following:

- (a) The written election of the Member to dissolve the Company;
- (b) The sale or disposition of substantially all Company assets, unless the Member elects to continue the Company's business;
- (c) Any event that makes it unlawful for the Company to continue its business;
- (d) Entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under the Act.

**13.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Member shall wind up the Company's affairs. The assets of the Company shall be applied and distributed in the following order:

1. Payment of debts and liabilities of the Company, including expenses of liquidation;
2. Establishment of any reserves deemed reasonably necessary for contingent or unforeseen liabilities;
3. Distribution of the remaining assets to the Member.

**13.3 Articles of Termination.** Upon completion of winding up, the Member shall file Articles of Termination with the Missouri Secretary of State.

---

## ARTICLE XIV — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**14.1 Amendments.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument executed by the Member.

**14.2 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Missouri, without regard to its conflict of law principles.

**14.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**14.4 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement of the Member with respect to the Company and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings, whether written or oral.

**14.5 Binding Effect.** This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Member and the Member's heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and assigns.

**14.6 Headings.** The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

**14.7 Certification of Authority.** The Member may execute and deliver a certificate stating that the Member is authorized to act on behalf of the Company. Any person dealing with the Company may rely on such certificate without further inquiry.
