Command AI

More structural authority and support-platform energy. Strongest "protect the perimeter, hold the line, stay calm under fire" read.

Phantom Operator

Sleeker and more spectral. Leans into stealth, precision, perceptiveness, and the ability to move through noise without adding any.

Balanced Master

The fusion path. Enough structure to feel dependable, enough phase/ghost energy to feel intelligent and elegant, and the best fit for an executive working partner.

Recommendation

Balanced Master is still the best base.

  • Keep the cyan/navy/violet palette.
  • Preserve the calm, high-trust expression.
  • If we refine further, push 10-15% more command geometry without losing the stealth elegance.
