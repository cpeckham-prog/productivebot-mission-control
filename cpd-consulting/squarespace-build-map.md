## Work section
**Headline:** Commercial systems for the messy middle of growth.

**Intro:** CPD is built for companies that have market pull and ambition, but need a more rigorous way to convert that pull into predictable revenue.

### Focus areas
1. **Go-to-market clarity** — Sharpen ICP, segmentation, messaging, channel priorities, and the practical path from current traction to a scalable motion.
2. **Revenue operating rhythm** — Build the meeting cadence, pipeline hygiene, scorecards, forecasting rules, and decision rights that keep teams aligned.
3. **Sales process & enablement** — Translate founder judgment into repeatable stages, qualification, discovery, mutual action plans, and manager coaching moments.
4. **CRM and data discipline** — Make the CRM usable: fewer fields, clearer definitions, better dashboards, and data that operators trust enough to act on.
5. **Forecasting & pipeline control** — Establish inspection habits and leading indicators so leaders can see risk earlier and choose interventions deliberately.
6. **AI-enabled operations** — Apply AI where it actually changes throughput: account research, call synthesis, rep follow-up, CRM updates, and executive visibility.
