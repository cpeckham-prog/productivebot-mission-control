## Work section
### Commercial systems for the messy middle of growth.

CPD is built for companies that have market pull and ambition, but need a more rigorous way to convert that pull into predictable execution.

### Go-to-market clarity
Sharpen ICP, segmentation, messaging, channel priorities, and the practical path from current traction to a scalable motion.

### Revenue operating rhythm
Build the meeting cadence, pipeline hygiene, scorecards, forecasting rules, and decision rights that keep teams aligned.

### Sales process & enablement
Translate founder judgment into repeatable stages, qualification, discovery, mutual action plans, and coaching moments.

### CRM and data discipline
Make the CRM usable: fewer fields, clearer definitions, better dashboards, and data that operators trust enough to act on.

### Forecasting & pipeline control
Establish inspection habits and leading indicators so leaders can see risk earlier and choose interventions deliberately.

### AI-enabled operations
Apply AI where it actually changes throughput: account research, call synthesis, follow-up, CRM updates, and executive visibility.
