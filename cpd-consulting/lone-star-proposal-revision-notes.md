## Comparable research snapshot

Sources reviewed via web search/fetch on May 8, 2026:

- Knex fractional CRO pricing guide: typical fractional CRO range listed at **$5,000-$15,000/month**; revenue strategy/GTM planning often **$4,000-$10,000/month**; pipeline management/forecasting **$2,000-$5,000/month**.
- PMGuru fractional executive cost guide: fractional executives often **$7,500-$25,000/month**; fractional CROs often **$12,000-$25,000/month**; common company range **$12,000-$18,000/month** for $10M-$100M companies.
- Red Letter X 90-Day Growth Foundation: 90-day GTM/sales system buildouts can promise full CRM, playbook, dashboards, outbound framework, and training at roughly **$10k-$25k** depending business size, but that type of scope is much heavier than Chris should promise in a first Lone Star engagement.

## Recommended positioning

Use this as the core verbal frame:

> “I don’t think the right first engagement is for me to come in and promise a complete revenue transformation in 90 days. The highest-value first step is to help Harrison get a clearer view of the commercial engine: what’s real in the pipeline, why deals stall after verbal yes, what needs to be true before CRM rollout, and what operating cadence gives him control without micromanaging the team.”

## Recommended price

Primary recommendation: **$10,000/month for 90 days = $30,000 total.**

Why:
- high enough to signal senior value
- easier to land as first CPD client than $37.5k
- supported by comparable fractional CRO/advisory ranges
- aligned with a bounded CEO advisory/blueprint scope
- avoids underpricing Chris into heavy implementation work

Possible alternate if Chris wants lower-friction close:

- **$7,500/month for 90 days = $22,500 total** for advisory-only, limited async support, no CRM implementation or extensive artifacts.

Possible stretch if Harrison wants deeper execution:

- **$12,500/month for 90 days = $37,500 total** if they want Chris more embedded in weekly deal reviews, CRM architecture, team coaching, and implementation oversight.

## Onsite / customer visit update

Chris asked to include a clean option for onsite work without making it feel like a cheap upsell menu. Added a section called **In-Person Working Sessions and Market Visits** under Engagement Cadence.

Positioning:
- onsite work is optional and purpose-driven, not a separate product
- possible uses: kickoff, end-of-engagement readout, monthly onsite working sessions, or select customer/prospect visits
- customer/prospect visits are framed around high-value portfolio opportunities, stalled late-stage deals, pending implementations, and case-study development
- travel is explicitly selective and tied to business outcomes

Travel/per diem research:
- Standard consulting practice supports client reimbursement of reasonable travel expenses: airfare/mileage, lodging, ground transport, parking, meals/incidental expenses.
- Per diem for meals/incidental expenses is standard and administratively cleaner than receipt-by-receipt meal reimbursement.
- IRS/GSA high-low 2025-2026 benchmark found: low-cost CONUS total per diem $225/day with $74 M&IE; high-cost localities $319/day with $86 M&IE. If lodging is separately reimbursed, use M&IE-only rates as the relevant per diem reference.
- Recommendation: proposal should not hard-code an exact per diem unless Chris wants to. Better: “aligned with prevailing IRS/GSA business travel guidance or mutually approved in advance.”
